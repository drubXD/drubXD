A thoughtful first step.

Pulse helps you organize your symptoms and health context before a conversation with a healthcare professional.

  1. Tell us how you feel.
  2. Add your age and relevant health history.
  3. Choose a location, if you’re comfortable.
  4. Review, request an AI assessment, and download your check-in.
AI-assisted information

With your permission, AI reads your answers and suggests possible explanations and next steps. These suggestions are not a diagnosis.

A symptom check cannot replace an examination, diagnostic tests, or professional medical care.

Your check-in stays here.

Answers stay in this page until you request an AI assessment and agree to share them. Then your symptoms, age, sex, health history, medications, exposures, additional notes, and typed city/country are sent through the Pulse server to Groq. Exact map coordinates are excluded.

Pulse does not write health answers or AI responses to a database, logs, or browser storage. Groq does not retain inference content by default, but temporary retention for reliability or abuse monitoring may apply. See the AI provider’s data handling policy. Refreshing or closing this page clears its in-memory answers. Downloads contain your health details and exact location if selected.

The map uses OpenStreetMap tiles and Leaflet from unpkg. Poppins loads from Google Fonts. These providers receive ordinary network information when content loads; map tile requests reveal the area viewed. Your health answers are not sent to these providers.
